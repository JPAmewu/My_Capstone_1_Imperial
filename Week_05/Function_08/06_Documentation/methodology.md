# Methodology

## Evidence boundary

Weeks 1-4 observed; Week 5 proposed only. The analysis reconstructs 44 observations from immutable starter arrays and the canonical ledger. The Week 5 query is held separately as a proposal. No outcome is imputed.

## Function-specific acquisition

Function 08 uses **GP-UCB**, as documented in `Week_05/02_Notebook/Week_5_Capstone.ipynb`. Decision record: Chosen using evidence through Week 4, before the Week 5 return.

This policy is adaptive and heuristic. It was selected using evidence available through Week 4; it is not a randomized or statistically controlled acquisition comparison. For Week 13, the reason was recorded before Week 13 outcomes existed.

## Validation

Inputs are finite and bounded; the proposal is distinct, capped at `0.999999`, and formatted as `0.110992-0.022977-0.255313-0.963757-0.895383-0.716713-0.030436-0.083412` with six decimals per coordinate. Results are descriptive within Function 08 and imply neither causality nor global optimality.
