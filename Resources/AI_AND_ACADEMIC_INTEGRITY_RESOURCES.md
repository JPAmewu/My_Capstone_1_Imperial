# AI and Academic Integrity Resources

This resource list supports the project's disclosure and responsible use of generative AI and AI-assisted software-development tools. It is supplementary to the project's formal [AI Use Disclosure](../Documentation/AI_USE_DISCLOSURE.md). Assessment-specific instructions from the programme or module take precedence over general guidance.

## Imperial College London guidance

### Generative AI guidance

Imperial College London's Library guidance explains appropriate academic use of generative AI, the need to verify AI-generated information, and expectations for acknowledging AI use in assessed work. It advises students to follow their department or course-specific requirements.

- https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/learning-support/generative-ai-guidance/

### Generative AI and Study Guidance Hub

Imperial's student guidance hub provides university-level information about generative AI in study and directs students to faculty and departmental guidance, recognising that requirements may differ between programmes and assessments.

- https://www.imperial.ac.uk/students/academic-support/ai-and-study-guidance-hub/

### Generative AI Principles

Imperial's Generative AI Principles provide a broader framework for thoughtful and informed use of generative AI in teaching, learning, and assessment.

- https://www.imperial.ac.uk/about/education/resources/ai-education-hub/generative-ai-principles/

### Centre for Academic English guidance on GenAI

The Centre for Academic English emphasises using generative AI to support rather than replace learning, maintaining human judgement and ownership, and critically evaluating AI-generated outputs.

- https://www.imperial.ac.uk/academic-english/cfae-guidance-on-genai/

## OpenAI tool resources

### Codex

OpenAI describes Codex as an AI coding agent that can assist with writing, reviewing, and shipping code. In this capstone, Codex was used as an AI-assisted development resource subject to human review and verification.

- https://help.openai.com/en/collections/14937394-codex

### Codex CLI — Getting Started

Technical guidance describing Codex CLI and its ability to read, modify, run, and help review code in a development environment.

- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11096431

## How these resources relate to this capstone

These sources provide background guidance and tool documentation; they are not evidence of the black-box optimisation results. The capstone's experimental evidence comes from the supplied datasets, submitted query points, authoritative returned objective values, and the validated repository records.

AI-generated explanations, code suggestions, and methodological recommendations were treated as material requiring review rather than authoritative academic or experimental evidence. The student remains responsible for understanding, verifying, and defending the final methodology, implementation, interpretation, and conclusions.

## Important assessment boundary

Permission to use generative AI is assessment-specific. The presence of these resources and the project's AI disclosure does not itself establish that any particular use is permitted. The applicable capstone brief, programme guidance, module instructions, and instructions from the module leader remain authoritative.
