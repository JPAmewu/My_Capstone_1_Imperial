# Methodology

## Evidence boundary

Weeks 1-9 observed; Week 10 proposed only. The analysis reconstructs 29 observations from immutable starter arrays and the canonical ledger. The Week 10 query is held separately as a proposal. No outcome is imputed.

## Function-specific acquisition

Function 05 uses **GP-UCB**, as documented in `Week_10/02_Notebook/Week_10_Capstone.ipynb`. Decision record: Chosen using evidence through Week 9, before the Week 10 return.

This policy is adaptive and heuristic. It was selected using evidence available through Week 9; it is not a randomized or statistically controlled acquisition comparison. For Week 13, the reason was recorded before Week 13 outcomes existed.

## Validation

Inputs are finite and bounded; the proposal is distinct, capped at `0.999999`, and formatted as `0.005552-0.918769-0.692571-0.988052` with six decimals per coordinate. Results are descriptive within Function 05 and imply neither causality nor global optimality.
