# Methodology

## Evidence boundary

Weeks 1-5 observed; Week 6 proposed only. The analysis reconstructs 35 observations from immutable starter arrays and the canonical ledger. The Week 6 query is held separately as a proposal. No outcome is imputed.

## Function-specific acquisition

Function 07 uses **GP-UCB**, as documented in `Week_06/02_Notebook/Week_6_Capstone.ipynb`. Decision record: Chosen using evidence through Week 5, before the Week 6 return.

This policy is adaptive and heuristic. It was selected using evidence available through Week 5; it is not a randomized or statistically controlled acquisition comparison. For Week 13, the reason was recorded before Week 13 outcomes existed.

## Validation

Inputs are finite and bounded; the proposal is distinct, capped at `0.999999`, and formatted as `0.042873-0.466831-0.379623-0.207090-0.367754-0.515646` with six decimals per coordinate. Results are descriptive within Function 07 and imply neither causality nor global optimality.
