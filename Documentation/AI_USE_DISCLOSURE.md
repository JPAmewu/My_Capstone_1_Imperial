# AI Use Disclosure

Generative AI and AI-assisted software-development tools were used as supporting resources during this capstone project. Their use supported learning, implementation, verification, and documentation while preserving the student's responsibility for the optimisation study.

## Tools and purposes

**ChatGPT (OpenAI)** was used to support conceptual discussion of Bayesian optimisation, Gaussian Processes, acquisition functions, exploration-exploitation trade-offs, interpretation of experimental results, debugging, and critical review of the developing methodology.

**Codex (OpenAI)** was used as an AI-assisted software-development tool for repository-level coding, refactoring, file organisation, validation, testing, documentation updates, and review of implementation issues.

This assistance includes the reader-facing final-report structure, generated
scoreboard and timeline code, reproducibility documentation, and repository
navigation improvements. Those artefacts summarise existing evidence; they do
not add or alter black-box observations.

## Human oversight

AI-generated code, explanations, and recommendations were treated as proposals requiring independent verification. Experimental evidence was derived from the supplied capstone data and authoritative black-box returns, not generated by an AI system.

The student reviewed methodological decisions, executed and tested the code, evaluated the optimisation results, and retained responsibility for accepting, modifying, or rejecting AI-generated recommendations.

No AI system was given access to the hidden objective functions. AI tools did
not generate portal returns, decide that an unreturned proposal was successful,
or replace the student's responsibility to understand and defend the work.

## Verification and evidence boundaries

AI-generated suggestions were not treated as experimental observations, authoritative black-box outputs, or proof of optimisation performance. Where AI-assisted code or methodological suggestions were adopted, they were checked against the capstone requirements, observed input-output data, executable code, diagnostic results, repository validation procedures, and supporting technical documentation.

The distinction between model proposals and observed evidence was maintained throughout the project: a proposed query was not treated as an observation until an authoritative objective value was returned and validated. AI tools did not supply or substitute for those objective values.

## Limitations

Generative AI systems may produce incorrect code, inaccurate explanations, inappropriate methodological recommendations, or apparently plausible statements that are not supported by evidence. AI outputs were therefore not treated as authoritative sources. Recommendations were independently reviewed and, where relevant, tested before incorporation into the repository.

Use of AI-assisted development can also make it more difficult to distinguish automated suggestions from the student's own methodological decisions unless the workflow is documented carefully. This disclosure is intended to make that assistance transparent while preserving a clear distinction between tool support, experimental evidence, and academic responsibility.

## Academic ownership and responsibility

The final repository represents the student's capstone work. Responsibility for the research workflow, methodological choices, experimental decisions, interpretation of results, validation of evidence, documentation, and conclusions remains with the student.

AI tools were used as supporting resources rather than as substitutes for understanding or academic judgement. The student remains responsible for being able to explain and defend the Bayesian optimisation methodology, the code and analytical choices adopted, the interpretation of the results, and the limitations reported in the project.

## Institutional guidance

Use of generative AI in assessed work remains subject to the applicable programme, module, and institutional requirements. This disclosure should therefore be read together with the assessment-specific guidance governing permitted AI use and acknowledgement.
