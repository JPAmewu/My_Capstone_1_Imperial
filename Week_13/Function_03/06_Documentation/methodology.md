# Methodology

## Evidence boundary

Weeks 1-12 observed; Week 13 proposed only. The analysis reconstructs 27 observations from immutable starter arrays and the canonical ledger. The Week 13 query is held separately as a proposal. No outcome is imputed. Legacy Week 11 arrays remain quarantined.

## Function-specific acquisition

Function 03 uses **PI**, as documented in `Week_13/02_Notebook/Week_13_Optimisation_Strategy.ipynb`. Decision record: The new best reinforces a stable structure and supports controlled exploitation.

This policy is adaptive and heuristic. It was selected using evidence available through Week 12; it is not a randomized or statistically controlled acquisition comparison. For Week 13, the reason was recorded before Week 13 outcomes existed.

## Validation

Inputs are finite and bounded; the proposal is distinct, capped at `0.999999`, and formatted as `0.685419-0.626770-0.366737` with six decimals per coordinate. Results are descriptive within Function 03 and imply neither causality nor global optimality.
