# Methodology

## Evidence boundary

Weeks 1-3 observed; Week 4 proposed only. The analysis reconstructs 43 observations from immutable starter arrays and the canonical ledger. The Week 4 query is held separately as a proposal. No outcome is imputed.

## Function-specific acquisition

Function 08 uses **GP-UCB**, as documented in `Week_04/02_Notebook/Week_4_Capstone.ipynb`. Decision record: Chosen using evidence through Week 3, before the Week 4 return.

This policy is adaptive and heuristic. It was selected using evidence available through Week 3; it is not a randomized or statistically controlled acquisition comparison. For Week 13, the reason was recorded before Week 13 outcomes existed.

## Validation

Inputs are finite and bounded; the proposal is distinct, capped at `0.999999`, and formatted as `0.356502-0.157574-0.172997-0.610561-0.106477-0.260297-0.411421-0.904740` with six decimals per coordinate. Results are descriptive within Function 08 and imply neither causality nor global optimality.
